I am a Software Engineer at **Google** in Mountain View, CA, working on user activity modeling and agentic recommendation systems.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at **The Ohio State University** (2026), advised by [Prof. Mi Zhang](https://mizhang.info/) in the AIoT-MLSys Lab as an OSU Graduate School University Fellow. My dissertation, _"Towards Supermemory in Spatial Computing: A Multimodal Approach for Memory Creation, Retrieval, and Visual Reasoning,"_ builds personal memory systems for augmented-reality smart glasses. Previously, I interned at **Meta Reality Labs** (2026) and **Google** (2025), earned my M.S. in CS at **Michigan State University** (2023), built wearable software at **Samsung R&D** (2018–2021), and co-founded [Bengali.AI](https://bengali.ai).
