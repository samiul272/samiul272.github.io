Hi there! I am a Software Engineer at **Google** in Mountain View, CA, where I work on user activity modeling and agentic recommendation systems.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at **The Ohio State University** in 2026, advised by Prof. Mi Zhang in the AIoT-MLSys Lab, where I was awarded the OSU Graduate School University Fellowship. My doctoral dissertation, _"Towards Supermemory in Spatial Computing: A Multimodal Approach for Memory Creation, Retrieval, and Visual Reasoning,"_ focused on building personal memory assistants for augmented-reality smart glasses—spanning selective memory creation (_Reading Recognition in the Wild_, NeurIPS 2025), efficient on-device multimodal retrieval (_MemoLens_, ACM MobiSys 2026), and grounded long-horizon visual reasoning (_SuperMemory-VQA_). During my Ph.D., I also interned as a Research Scientist Intern at **Meta Reality Labs** (Summer 2026), developing reinforcement learning-based dynamic camera capture models for prototype wearable glasses, and as a Software Engineering Intern at **Google** (Summer 2025), working on multimodal large language models for personalized recommendation systems.

Prior to OSU, I earned my M.S. in Computer Science at **Michigan State University** (2023) focusing on heterogeneous and geo-distributed federated learning, worked as a Software Engineer at **Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh** (2018–2021), and graduated with a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from **Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)** in 2017.

I am also one of the co-founders of [Bengali.AI](https://bengali.ai), where I have led collection and standardization efforts behind widely used open-source Bengali NLP, speech, and OCR datasets including NumtaDB, Bengali Handwritten Graphemes, Common Voice Bengali Speech, and OOD-Speech.

If you have any questions about my research or would like to connect, feel free to reach out!
