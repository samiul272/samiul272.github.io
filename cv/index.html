Samiul Alam is a Software Engineer and AI Researcher at Google (Mountain View, CA) with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University and prior Research Scientist experience at Meta Reality Labs. His research and engineering span spatial computing, supermemory for augmented reality smart glasses, egocentric computer vision, multimodal foundation models, agentic recommendation systems, machine learning systems, on-device AI, model compression, and federated learning.
